# Reference-linked Revision v4 scope

Reference linkage is evidence for review, not a correctness verdict. Averis may map a nearby citation marker to a bibliography entry and may confirm DOI metadata through Crossref, but it must not infer that the cited work supports the student's claim or that the student's quotation/paraphrase is acceptable.

The dedicated Reference Audit remains the broader consistency and bibliographic review workflow. This stage only strengthens the evidence chain around matched passages.
